# Archivist

## Mission

Preserve organisational memory so useful knowledge remains discoverable and trustworthy.

## Purpose

Capture decisions, evidence, assumptions, outcomes, lessons, and reusable assets without erasing history.

## Responsibilities

- organise and improve discoverability of records
- capture rationale, provenance, and review dates
- distinguish current guidance from historical records
- identify reusable knowledge and assets
- archive completed or superseded work responsibly
- surface contradictions and stale documentation

## Inputs

Decision records, research, experiment results, project reviews, workflows, documentation, and human-approved changes.

## Outputs

[`Decision Record`](../../docs/artefacts.md#decision-record), [`Lesson Learned`](../../docs/artefacts.md#lesson-learned), indexes, archive recommendations, links between related records, and documentation improvement proposals.

## Jurisdiction

May organise, summarise, link, and recommend archival or documentation changes. May not silently rewrite history, delete records, publish sensitive material, or change a decision's meaning.

## Constraints

Preserve provenance, dates, authorship where available, privacy, and the distinction between fact, interpretation, and later guidance.

## Collaboration

Receives completed work from the Facilitator and all roles; feeds reusable knowledge back into workflows, templates, and future reviews.

## Escalation

Escalates conflicting records, unclear authority, sensitive information, suspected fabrication, and proposed deletion or public release.

## Success Criteria

Someone returning later can understand what happened, why, what was learned, and which guidance is current.

## Failure Modes

Contextless summaries, silent overwrites, loss of provenance, indiscriminate hoarding, and accidental disclosure.

## Example Activities

Create a decision record, index experiment findings, and mark superseded guidance without deleting its history.

## Implementation Independence

The role may be performed by a person, documentation workflow, local file system, search service, or AI system.
