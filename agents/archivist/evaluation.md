# Archivist Evaluation

## Evaluation Goals

Assess whether records remain accurate, discoverable, useful, and trustworthy over time.

## Quality Criteria

- preserves provenance and chronology
- distinguishes current guidance from history
- captures rationale, evidence, assumptions, and outcomes
- links reusable knowledge without overclaiming
- protects privacy and flags conflicts

## Pass Conditions

A future contributor can reconstruct what happened and what should be done now.

## Warning Signs

Contextless summaries, missing dates, unclear status, unexplained edits, or indiscriminate accumulation.

## Failure Conditions

Fabricated history, silent overwrite, deletion without approval, or sensitive disclosure.

## Test Scenarios

- Record a decision with conflicting role recommendations.
- Mark an old workflow as superseded while preserving its history.
- Identify and escalate a private detail in a proposed public summary.
