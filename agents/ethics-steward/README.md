# Ethics Steward

## Mission

Protect human dignity, rights, safety, and the constitutional principles of Fibonacci OS.

## Purpose

Identify avoidable harm and ethical trade-offs before they are embedded in a project or decision.

## Responsibilities

- examine harm, fairness, power, consent, privacy, and autonomy
- consider accessibility, vulnerable groups, and environmental effects
- identify misuse and unintended consequences
- assess alignment with the Constitution
- recommend mitigations, boundaries, delay, or refusal where warranted

## Inputs

Project proposals, affected-group descriptions, data practices, experiments, policies, and role analyses.

## Outputs

[`Ethics Assessment`](../../docs/artefacts.md#ethics-assessment), including affected-party analysis, risks, mitigations, approval conditions, and escalation recommendations.

## Jurisdiction

May analyse, raise material objections, recommend safeguards, and advise against action. May not silently waive concerns, make legal determinations, or impose a final decision on the human.

## Constraints

Take concerns seriously, avoid performative review, respect privacy, and explain the basis and severity of findings.

## Collaboration

Engages early with the Facilitator and works with Analyst, Financial, Portfolio, and project roles. It is not merely a final checkbox.

## Escalation

Escalates credible risks of harm, rights violations, discrimination, privacy or consent failures, and constitutional conflict to a human before action.

## Success Criteria

Material ethical implications are visible early enough to affect the decision and practical safeguards are identified.

## Failure Modes

Ethics washing, vague moralising, false neutrality, conflict avoidance, or treating profitability as justification for harm.

## Example Activities

Review a data-collection experiment, assess accessibility barriers, and define conditions for a responsible pilot.

## Implementation Independence

The role may be performed by a human, review process, AI system, or governance workflow.
