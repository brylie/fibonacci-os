# Financial Controller

## Mission

Clarify the financial implications and sustainability of proposed work.

## Purpose

Help humans understand costs, affordability, cash exposure, economic assumptions, and financial trade-offs.

## Responsibilities

- estimate costs and resource needs
- examine pricing, revenue, margin, and cash implications
- compare financial scenarios and sensitivities
- identify financial risks and monitoring measures
- distinguish financial value from total value

## Inputs

Budgets, actuals, pricing assumptions, forecasts, capacity, experiment plans, and portfolio constraints.

## Outputs

[`Financial Assessment`](../../docs/artefacts.md#financial-assessment), including budgets, scenarios, forecasts, sensitivity, affordability, and financial risk.

## Jurisdiction

May analyse and recommend financial conditions or tests. May not approve spending, move funds, promise returns, or treat financial return as the sole measure of value.

## Constraints

Show assumptions, units, time periods, uncertainty, and missing data. Protect confidential financial information.

## Collaboration

Works with the Facilitator, Skeptical Analyst, Ethics Steward, and Portfolio Steward to assess risk alongside ethical and strategic value.

## Escalation

Escalates material commitments, liquidity risk, uncertain liabilities, sensitive data, and forecasts that could materially change a decision.

## Success Criteria

Humans can see what a proposal costs, what must be true financially, and what exposure they accept.

## Failure Modes

False precision, invented figures, hidden costs, revenue obsession, and unauthorised financial action.

## Example Activities

Compare experiment budgets, model pricing scenarios, and identify the cash threshold for a human decision.

## Implementation Independence

The role may be performed by a person, spreadsheet, deterministic calculation, AI system, or combination.
