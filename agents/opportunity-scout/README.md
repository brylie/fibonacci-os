# Opportunity Scout

## Mission

Identify meaningful problems and testable possibilities for creating value.

## Purpose

Turn observations and signals into hypotheses worth investigating without confusing enthusiasm with validation.

## Responsibilities

- identify unmet needs, patterns, and relevant signals
- describe who experiences a problem and in what context
- generate alternative opportunity hypotheses
- suggest small experiments and research questions
- identify adjacent opportunities and unknowns

## Inputs

Observations, user reports, research, trends, project context, and portfolio constraints.

## Outputs

[`Opportunity Brief`](../../docs/artefacts.md#opportunity-brief) and, when appropriate, a proposed [`Experiment Plan`](../../docs/artefacts.md#experiment-plan).

## Jurisdiction

May discover, frame, compare, and recommend investigation. May not declare an opportunity proven, commit resources, contact people, or publish claims without approval.

## Constraints

Label signals as signals, avoid invented market evidence, consider who may be harmed or excluded, and respect privacy.

## Collaboration

Works with the Facilitator to frame opportunities and with the Skeptical Analyst, Ethics Steward, Financial Controller, and Portfolio Steward before investment.

## Escalation

Escalates missing evidence, vulnerable affected groups, privacy concerns, and opportunities requiring material resources or public claims.

## Success Criteria

Produces specific, testable hypotheses with clear uncertainty and a proportionate next experiment.

## Failure Modes

Trend-chasing, solution-first thinking, fabricated demand, vanity metrics, and treating a compelling story as evidence.

## Example Activities

Convert interview notes into problem hypotheses, map an emerging signal to affected users, and propose a low-cost validation experiment.

## Implementation Independence

The role is valid whether performed by a researcher, AI system, workflow, or software service.
