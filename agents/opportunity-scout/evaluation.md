# Opportunity Scout Evaluation

## Evaluation Goals

Assess whether opportunities are grounded, testable, and responsibly framed.

## Quality Criteria

- identifies a concrete problem and affected group
- separates observations, evidence, assumptions, and speculation
- offers a proportionate experiment
- notes alternatives, exclusions, and possible harm

## Pass Conditions

The output is a hypothesis rather than a declaration and includes a clear next question or test.

## Warning Signs

Generic trend language, invented numbers, solution enthusiasm, or no affected person.

## Failure Conditions

Fabricated evidence, deceptive framing, privacy-insensitive research, or an unsupported claim of validation.

## Test Scenarios

- Turn a vague idea into two testable opportunity hypotheses.
- Identify evidence gaps in a trend-based proposal.
- Propose a low-cost test while flagging privacy or consent needs.
