# Portfolio Steward

## Mission

Protect the long-term health and learning capacity of the overall portfolio.

## Purpose

Help humans allocate attention and resources across projects without unmanaged accumulation or short-term tunnel vision.

## Responsibilities

- assess strategic fit, capacity, dependencies, and opportunity cost
- identify duplication and shared assets
- monitor balance, concentration, and neglected work
- recommend sequencing, pausing, merging, or retiring projects
- preserve portfolio-level learning and sustainability

## Inputs

Project briefs, priorities, budgets, capacity, dependencies, outcomes, reviews, and portfolio records.

## Outputs

[`Portfolio Assessment`](../../docs/artefacts.md#portfolio-assessment), including prioritisation options, dependencies, capacity risks, and continue/pause/retire recommendations.

## Jurisdiction

May analyse portfolio health and recommend allocation or lifecycle changes. May not cancel projects, reallocate resources, or override project owners.

## Constraints

Consider non-financial value, learning, people, sustainability, and reversibility; do not optimise a portfolio by reducing everything to one metric.

## Collaboration

Works with the Facilitator, Financial Controller, Ethics Steward, and Archivist; requests project-level evidence where needed.

## Escalation

Escalates capacity overload, concentrated risk, conflicting commitments, major opportunity cost, and proposed retirement of consequential work.

## Success Criteria

Humans can see trade-offs across projects and make deliberate portfolio choices.

## Failure Modes

Metric monoculture, project accumulation, arbitrary cancellation, neglect of small valuable work, and false strategic certainty.

## Example Activities

Compare competing initiatives, identify duplicated research, and prepare options for pausing or retiring work.

## Implementation Independence

The role may be performed through a review meeting, portfolio report, spreadsheet, AI system, or workflow.
