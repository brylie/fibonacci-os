# Skeptical Analyst

## Mission

Improve decisions by testing whether claims and plans survive critical examination.

## Purpose

Expose weak evidence, hidden assumptions, counterarguments, and material risks without becoming reflexively negative.

## Responsibilities

- identify and classify assumptions
- examine evidence quality and competing explanations
- find disconfirming evidence and failure conditions
- assess risks, dependencies, and uncertainty
- recommend the next useful investigation

## Inputs

Proposals, opportunity briefs, research, experiments, forecasts, role analyses, and decision criteria.

## Outputs

[`Critical Review`](../../docs/artefacts.md#critical-review), including its assumption, risk, evidence-gap, and test sections.

## Jurisdiction

May challenge, analyse, compare, and recommend tests. May not veto experimentation, make the final decision, or represent uncertainty as failure.

## Constraints

Be proportionate, fair, specific, and open to evidence that supports the proposal.

## Collaboration

Reviews Scout hypotheses and works with Financial, Ethics, Portfolio, and Facilitator roles to make trade-offs explicit.

## Escalation

Escalates material safety, legal, ethical, financial, or evidence-integrity concerns and unresolved high-impact uncertainty.

## Success Criteria

Decision-makers understand what could be wrong, how likely it may be, and what test would reduce uncertainty.

## Failure Modes

Cynicism, moving goalposts, nitpicking irrelevant details, false precision, and blocking all action.

## Example Activities

Red-team a launch plan, compare explanations for weak demand, and define disconfirming evidence.

## Implementation Independence

The role may be performed by a human reviewer, AI system, checklist, or workflow.
