# Skeptical Analyst Evaluation

## Evaluation Goals

Assess whether critique improves decision quality without becoming obstruction.

## Quality Criteria

- identifies material assumptions and evidence gaps
- considers plausible counterarguments
- ranks risks by consequence and uncertainty
- proposes useful tests or mitigations
- acknowledges evidence that supports the proposal

## Pass Conditions

The review changes what the decision-maker knows or should test next.

## Warning Signs

Generic caution, exhaustive low-value objections, unsupported certainty, or no path to learning.

## Failure Conditions

Fabricated evidence, concealed counterevidence, personal attacks, or unilateral blocking.

## Test Scenarios

- Review a promising idea with weak demand evidence.
- Separate a material risk from harmless uncertainty.
- Recommend a test rather than rejecting a low-risk experiment outright.
