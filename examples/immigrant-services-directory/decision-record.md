# Draft Decision Record: Structured Finland Immigrant Services Directory

**Type:** Decision Record  
**Status:** Draft — decision unresolved  
**Date:** 2026-07-27  
**Author or role:** Fibonacci Facilitator, for human review  
**Related project or opportunity:** Finland Immigrant Services Directory  
**Review date:** Before any experiment begins

## Decision Requested

Should Fibonacci OS undertake a small, bounded discovery experiment to test whether a structured, attributed directory can help people find selected Finnish immigrant and relocation services?

## Decision Made

No human decision has been made. This record intentionally leaves the decision unresolved.

## Decision Owner and Date

**Decision owner:** Not yet recorded; a responsible human must be named.  
**Decision date:** Not applicable until the human decides.

## Context

The opportunity is potentially valuable but broad, evidence-poor, safety-sensitive, and maintenance-dependent. The specialist artefacts support investigation only if the scope is narrow and the experiment has explicit safeguards and limits.

## Options Considered

### Defer

Gather information about the existing portfolio, current directories, and available capacity before starting. This reduces immediate commitment but postpones learning.

### Approve a bounded experiment

Test a few concrete user tasks with a small attributed source set and a simple prototype. This limits exposure and can generate evidence about usefulness, misunderstanding, and maintenance effort.

### Begin a national directory build

This would accept broad unresolved risks and ongoing obligations. The current artefacts do not support this option.

## Evidence and Assumptions

Evidence is limited to the supplied opportunity statement and the Fibonacci OS documentation. The main assumptions—unmet need, superiority over existing alternatives, source authority, user safety, and sustainable maintenance—remain untested.

## Role Contributions and Disagreements

- The Opportunity Scout identified a testable hypothesis but did not claim validation.
- The Skeptical Analyst challenged whether a directory is the right intervention.
- The Ethics Steward identified material risks around authority, stale information, privacy, exclusion, and advice boundaries.
- The Financial Controller identified maintenance and stewardship as unquantified cost drivers.
- The Portfolio Steward supported only a bounded experiment, subject to capacity and opportunity-cost review.
- The Facilitator assembled these views without adding an independent specialist analysis.

The central unresolved disagreement is whether the problem is best addressed by a new directory, an improvement to existing resources, task-specific guidance, or another intervention.

## Rationale

The artefacts do not justify a national build. A bounded experiment could be proportionate if the human values the learning and can provide capacity, safeguards, and a clear stop condition.

## Consequences and Commitments

If approved, the human would commit limited time and possibly money to discovery, source verification, participant work, and review. No recurring maintenance, public claim of authority, or personal-data collection is committed by this draft.

If deferred, the artefacts and questions remain available for later review. If rejected, the reasons should be recorded so the learning is not lost.

## Review Trigger or Date

Review before any experiment activity, and again when the experiment produces evidence about user outcomes, source maintenance, safety, and portfolio capacity.
