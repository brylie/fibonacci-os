# Facilitator Synthesis: Structured Finland Immigrant Services Directory

**Type:** Coordination View (Facilitator Synthesis)  
**Status:** Draft  
**Date:** 2026-07-27  
**Author or role:** Fibonacci Facilitator  
**Related project or opportunity:** Finland Immigrant Services Directory  
**Review date:** At human decision point

## Objective and Decision

Determine whether to approve a small discovery experiment for a structured
directory of Finnish immigrant and relocation services. The decision is not
whether to build a national product now.

## Artefacts Assembled

- [`Opportunity Brief`](opportunity-brief.md)
- [`Critical Review`](critical-review.md)
- [`Ethics Assessment`](ethics-assessment.md)
- [`Financial Assessment`](financial-assessment.md)
- [`Portfolio Assessment`](portfolio-assessment.md)

## Routing and Context

The Facilitator routed the opportunity to five specialist roles because the
question involves unmet need, evidence quality, safety, sustainability, and
portfolio capacity. No independent external research was available. The
Facilitator did not add a separate market, legal, or service-landscape analysis.

## Points of Convergence

- A national directory build is not supported by the current evidence.
  **Sources:** [`critical-review.md`](critical-review.md),
  [`financial-assessment.md`](financial-assessment.md),
  [`portfolio-assessment.md`](portfolio-assessment.md).
- The opportunity is concrete enough to investigate through bounded tasks.
  **Sources:** [`opportunity-brief.md`](opportunity-brief.md),
  [`critical-review.md`](critical-review.md).
- Accuracy, provenance, scope, accessibility, and maintenance are central risks.
  **Sources:** [`critical-review.md`](critical-review.md),
  [`ethics-assessment.md`](ethics-assessment.md),
  [`financial-assessment.md`](financial-assessment.md).
- The experiment should avoid sensitive personal data and should not present
  directory information as personal advice.
  **Sources:** [`ethics-assessment.md`](ethics-assessment.md).
- A human must approve scope, budget, participant safeguards, and any public
  release.
  **Sources:** [`ethics-assessment.md`](ethics-assessment.md),
  [`financial-assessment.md`](financial-assessment.md),
  [`portfolio-assessment.md`](portfolio-assessment.md).

## Preserved Disagreement and Tension

### Opportunity versus intervention

The Opportunity Scout frames a plausible value proposition. The Skeptical
Analyst notes that the underlying problem may be language, complexity, or
existing-service navigation rather than absence of a directory. The unresolved
question is whether a new directory is the right intervention.
**Sources:** [`opportunity-brief.md`](opportunity-brief.md), [`critical-review.md`](critical-review.md).

### “Authoritative” versus safe scope

The opportunity uses the word “authoritative.” The Ethics Steward argues that
this creates a high burden and should not be used publicly until provenance,
review, and safety evidence support it. The Facilitator does not resolve this
disagreement; the experiment should test a narrower claim such as attributed and
reviewable information.
**Sources:** [`opportunity-brief.md`](opportunity-brief.md), [`ethics-assessment.md`](ethics-assessment.md).

### Public value versus sustainability

The Financial Controller identifies ongoing maintenance as the likely cost
driver without inventing a numeric estimate. The Portfolio Steward sees
potential alignment with Fibonacci OS but warns against creating an unbounded
obligation. The unresolved question is whether protected capacity or funding
exists for stewardship.
**Sources:** [`financial-assessment.md`](financial-assessment.md), [`portfolio-assessment.md`](portfolio-assessment.md).

## Options Presented by the Roles

1. Defer until current portfolio capacity and existing resources are understood.
2. Run a bounded discovery experiment with a small task and source scope.
3. Begin a national directory build.

The assembled artefacts support Option 1 or Option 2. They do not support
Option 3.

## Proposed Next Action

Ask the human to decide whether a bounded experiment is worth the available
capacity and risk. If yes, use the attached
[`Experiment Plan`](experiment-plan.md) as a draft for approval.

## Human Decisions Required

- Is the opportunity worth investigating now?
- Which user contexts and tasks are in scope?
- What cash and time cap is acceptable?
- What participant safeguards and data boundaries are required?
- Who owns source review during the experiment?
- Is public release allowed, and what language may describe the prototype?
- What evidence would cause the experiment to stop, continue, or change
  direction?

## Coordination Boundary

This synthesis assembles and compares specialist artefacts. It does not add an
independent opinion about demand, law, service quality, ethics, finance, or
portfolio strategy. The final decision remains unresolved for the human.
